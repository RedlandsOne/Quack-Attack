# 🦆 Quack Atack

> A suspicious little duck. A rising water level. One very strange program.

**Quack Atack** is a small experimental desktop app featuring a duck floating on water while the water slowly rises.

As the water rises, the duck gets closer and closer to being submerged. Press **`,`** to reset the water back to the bottom and start the cycle again.

## Features

- 🦆 Animated duck floating on water
- 🌊 Slowly rising water
- 🔄 Press **`,`** to reset the water
- 💻 Lightweight and simple
- 🦠 Designed with a strange "virus" aesthetic
- 🎲 Completely pointless, but oddly entertaining

## How It Works

1. Launch **Quack Atack**.
2. The duck appears on the water.
3. The water slowly begins to rise.
4. Eventually, the water reaches the duck.
5. Press **`,`**.
6. The water drops back to the bottom.
7. Repeat.
